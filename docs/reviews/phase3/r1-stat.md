## Checked and found sound
- Gap law without afterpulsing. I re-derived it independently: the record at tau_np is isolated, so gaps are renewal. g(u) = a S(u) with S' = -a S(. - tau_p) holds because the window (u - tau_p, u) is independent of qualifying points before u - tau_p. The CDF 1 - S(u + tau_p) is right, and the dominant root lambda = n for n tau_p <= 1 (W0 otherwise) is right. Accurate to < 3e-5 for n tau_p <= 0.5 or >= 2.
- Afterpulse terms. I re-derived all three bracket terms (recorded afterpulse; blocked afterpulse restarting the paralyzable stage, q(t) = g0(t - tau_p)). The law matches simulation (chi-square on 8e5 gaps, p >= 0.17) for tau_a < tau_p, tau_a = tau_p, tau_a > tau_p, and tau_p = 0, while pending afterpulses are rare. The probabilities sum to 1.
- Edge density for tau_np: a + p e^{-n tau_p}/tau_a. The tick-quantile interval on the summed density (n = 1, f0 = sum N_i f_i(0)) is right for the minimum over all gaps. tau_np coverage was 95-100% in every probe.
- Binning: tick-aligned edges, an exact CDF per bin, geometric tail bins. The per-run 1-D Newton profile is path-independent (cached vs fresh: 3e-5 in NLL).
- Hard-edge case and upper limit. True tau_p = 0, 2 runs x about 2-6k gaps: false identification 4/200 (about the 2.5% expected one-sided at 3.84), upper limits covered. tau_p = 300 ps: 97/100 unidentified, 98/100 covered.
- Coverage in other regimes (100-200 replicates): series-spad 95/100 (K=8 and K=40 identical); tau_p 45 / tau_np 50 ns 92/100; tau_p 2 ns at 1e7/3e7 95/100; few gaps (2 runs, 1 ms) 192/200; 2 Mcps dark plus 300 ps jitter 91/100; unequal durations 95/100 (the estimate is fine; only status is wrong, finding 1).
- Soft (gradual) recovery is flagged as a misfit (ppc 1e-58 to 1e-268), as the docstring claims.
- The Pearson dof of G - 1 (ignoring per-run normalisation and fitted parameters) is conservative, as stated.
