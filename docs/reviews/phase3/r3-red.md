## Checked and found sound

- Artifact integrity: the file name must equal the SHA-256 of canonical JCS bytes, the posterior hash and
  shape are checked, `status` must agree with `ppc_pvalue`, and summary values must lie in their intervals.
  Editing the JSON without re-minting is refused. Re-minting (for example, dropping `inputs` to get around
  disjointness) is possible, as `read_artifact` documents (no signatures). Not re-reported.
- Policy: loosened rules must go through `allow` with a reason (`__post_init__` rebuilds the rules and
  rejects subclasses), the fingerprint is pinned, and unknown correction names are refused. The CLI scopes
  `--allow` to the setup's analysis. Overrides and their reasons are in the result. The CLI prints only
  "(modified)" plus the "policy modified" label.
- `--allow misfit_forward_model`: the result gets `FORWARD_MISFIT_ACCEPTED` and "policy modified", and the
  raw `CAPTURE_IMBALANCE` label is computed independently and stays. The same label covers the Fix-5 drift
  refusal, where the wording "the predictive check failed" is slightly off but still visible.
- Misfit calibrations: refused unless `misfit_calibration` is allowed; then labelled.
- A bounded dead time with a dead fraction above 20% is refused (seen on hom-snspd). Finding 1 shows how
  another detector's artifacts get around this.
- Disjointness: whole tag-array and file hashes (slices are limited by the earlier L4, not re-reported).
- Deadtime-series artifacts are skipped with `SERIES_UNUSED` for HOM, fringe and CHSH, and are not cited.
- Raw headline values are not affected by any calibration choice. Every attack above moves only the
  secondary corrected value, or the record's description of it.
