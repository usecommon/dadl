# Performance: cost of phase 3 at realistic data sizes and in CI (round 9)

HEAD 2e15eba, branch m4/series-deadtime. Apple M5 Pro (18 cores, 64 GB), NumPy 2.5.4, one BLAS thread.
Probes, data and the patch are in `scratchpad/council/r9-perf/`. The probe patch is `perf.patch`: it applies to a
copy of `python/` in `r9-perf/patched/`, and the repo is untouched. "Python backend" means `DADL_BACKEND=python`, the
pure-NumPy reference. Every CI gate job installs only `./python[test]`, so the gates run that backend. "Rust" means the
compiled `dadl_core` installed in the venv.

  the 20-concurrent-job limit (max concurrency observed 20), so wall time tracks total job-minutes. The gate jobs

  with the Rust core. With the Python backend (as CI runs it) it takes 390 s locally on HEAD and 270 s on the patched tree (-31%).
- HEAD run 44781778595 (in progress when read, about 17:45 UTC): **chsh-spad shard 3/4 took 27.7 min** (17:17:03-17:44:47), against 11.0 min at round 7 in run 44765048652. ## Checked and found sound

- The joint draws and the forward Monte Carlo (`propagate.CalibrationSet.draws`, `forward.corrected_chsh` at N_MC
  4,000) are a small share of every profile (below the top 45 entries). They are not a hot spot at any size.
- Series dead-time calibration cost is set by its binning (at most 6,000 uniform bins plus geometric ones), not by
  tag count: 0.6-1.4 s (series-spad) and about 15 s (passive-quench) from 2.5e5 to 2.5e7 gaps. Memory is about 3x the
  gap bytes.
- The Rust kernels (`grid_coincidences`, `cross_correlation`, `occupied_slots`) are linear and allocate at most about
  1x the inputs per call. `histogram.cross_correlation` chunks its pairs (`_CHUNK_PAIRS`).
- The gate harnesses spawn per-replicate `SeedSequence` children and chunk work across a `ProcessPoolExecutor`. Shards
  and jobs do not change results, and BLAS is pinned to one thread in CI, which is right for 4 workers on 4 vCPUs.
- `shifted_coincidences` computes all whole-window offsets from one cross-correlation histogram rather than 200
  intersections.
- The patch keeps every analysed value, label and count identical on both backends (pickled `chsh` results at 1e5,
  1e6 and 1e7; result hashes over 3-24 propagation replicates per regime; `calibrate_deadtime_series` reprs).
  Unit suite on the patched tree, Python backend: 566 passed, 1 failed. The failure is `test_method_versions::test_method_code_changes_come_with_a_new_version`, the intended guard: `deadtime.py` and `accidentals.py` are hashed method code. Landing #1 or #6 means re-pinning the hashes with a note that outputs are unchanged, or bumping the version. The patch also skips 15 more tests, because the archive copy lacks repo-only files.
