HEAD 2e15eba (m4/series-deadtime), repo venv, every run from the scratch dir `r9-red/`. Every number below comes
from the CLI (`.venv/bin/dadl`) or the package's own functions. "Recorded" tags are DADL-simulated tags re-saved with

## Checked and found sound

- Every override leaves a per-event label when its check fires. `misfit_forward_model` gives
  `FORWARD_MISFIT_ACCEPTED`, `DELAY_DRIFT_ACCEPTED` and `OFFPEAK_ACCEPTED` apart, and each is printed under the
  corrected value. `misfit_calibration` gives `MISFIT_ACCEPTED`, attached only when the misfit artifact is used.
  Overrides whose check does not fire add nothing.
- `--condition gaps=1` on `calibrate dark` cannot loosen the exponential-law floor: a clash with a fit-recorded name
  is refused (`dark._merge_conditions`).
- Dead-time bounds and the zero-to-bound range are conservative for every analysis when the bound really is a bound.
  At the bound, CHSH |E| is lowest, and HOM and fringe V are lowest (the conditional term reduces the high-coincidence
  acquisition). The jitter bound is also conservative for HOM. A single-channel live-fraction error leaves E unchanged
  for a symmetric outcome table.
- The downward gap test, `DEAD_TIME_ABOVE_GAPS` and the CHSH dead-time-selection estimate use count rules or
  quantiles, so isolated short gaps do not move them. Only the upward test (Finding 1) uses `gaps[0]`.
- `calibrate deadtime`: one stray short gap is removed (`np.delete` of the minimum), and two or more echoes produce
  an empty 50 ns-1 us region that the predictive check rejects (fails closed).
- Pulsed light (T2 or T3) never makes the off-peak estimate too high: the central window holds the same-pulse
  accidentals that on-comb side windows hold. Undetected pulsed cases bias V and |E| low (Finding 3), not across a
  threshold.
- Validity: an artifact without `valid_for` is labelled; with one, a missing `--acquired` is refused. The detector
  serial is required for hardware setups and compared at use.

Not tested: Finding 1 with delay-jitter artifacts (the forward check). The echoes sit 50 ns from the pair peaks,
outside the window histogram, so the check should not see them, but this was not run.
