## Checked and found sound

- CHSH: corrected S is printed and recorded as secondary ("secondary, not a Bell claim (the headline is raw S)"); the default policy refuses every correction and the record keeps the overrides with their reasons and the policy fingerprint; "threshold crossed only after correction" exists for all three analyses.
- The CHSH calibration part is about right: 40 independent sets of four 0.2 s pair calibrations on the same tags give a point spread of 0.00159 against a mean reported calibration sd of 0.00136 (ratio 1.17, within the about 11% sampling error of 40 sds; `probes/calsd.py`). The quickstart's 2.6918 against the CLI's 2.6950 on the same tags is a 1.9 sd draw of that spread.
- Fitted values recover the injected truth in my runs: dark rates, afterpulse, dead time (chsh-spad), combined jitter 494 ps for 350 x sqrt 2, HOM corrected 0.9198 for 0.920, fringe 0.9508 for 0.95; the corrected point is independent of the Monte Carlo seed and `monte_carlo` records seed and draws.
- Provenance guards: files not marked simulated are refused under `backend: sim`; the CLI labels simulated input and records input hashes.
- Artifacts state `uncertainty.not_included` and "statistical (counting) only"; corrections whose bound gives a dead fraction > 20% are refused with the range explained.
- CHSH sign convention matches the declared angles (E(a,b') negative at 0/67.5 deg).
