# Detector physics (SPAD / SNSPD dead time, M4)

Probe scripts: `scratchpad/council/r1-phys/` (`probes.py soft|twilight|apdiode|tagjit`, `tw2.py`, `soft2.py`,
`aqreset.py`, `mod.py`, `mod2.py`, `low.py`, `edge.py`, `ppc.py`, `cli/`). All of them call the package's
own `calibrate_deadtime_series`, `detect`, `series_measured_rate`. Custom detector models (soft recharge,
twilight, AQ reset window, intensity modulation) are written in the probe and fed to the package fit.

## Checked and found sound

- The series chain in `detect`, `_self_exciting` and `_coupled` matches the code's statement: every candidate (photon, dark count, afterpulse, crosstalk) restarts tau_p, and pending candidates win ties.
- The tau_np edge is honest when there is no tagger jitter: the mean excess of the shortest gap over the truth is 9 ps, and P(excess ≥ 46 ps) = 1.0% over 200 seeds (`edge.py`). The 22046 [22023, 22053] seen once in `low.py` is that 1% tail.
- Twilight counts (AQ hold-off 22 ns; photons in the last 0.2-2 ns recorded at the reset end): a small window (0.2 ns) gives a tau_p upper limit and passes, while a window of 0.5-2 ns at Mcps is flagged (ppc 2e-3 to 1e-275). Neither gives a confident wrong tau_p. Only the README's crosstalk paragraph says "twilight not modelled"; the deadtime docs could add that it shows as a misfit (see #2).
- A soft passive-quench recharge (RC 15 ns, threshold 0.3, electronics 10 ns) is flagged (ppc 4e-60).
- The CLI path (`simulate --deadtime-scan`, `calibrate dark`, `calibrate deadtime`) on chsh-spad works end to end. With no paralysis it reports upper limits and the afterpulse sensitivity notes.
- A ppc of exactly 1 is the Bonferroni combination with the interruption test (`min(1, 2·min(p))`), not a degenerate Pearson statistic (raw p 0.65-0.76 in `ppc.py`).
- Placing afterpulses at the dead-time end plus Exp(tau_a) is the memoryless equivalent of single-trap release (p is then the post-dead-time probability). It is consistent between the simulator, the dark fit and the series fit.
- The forward live-fraction form (1 - m_a·tau_a)(1 - m_b·tau_b)(1 + C·min tau) is exact to first order for NP Poisson input. Ignoring tau_p is a second-order effect (finding #4 gives its size).
- SNSPD soft recovery and latching are kept out and stated, and the SNSPD dead time stays an upper limit.
