# Series dead time after rounds 1-3: branch search, joined interval, use-time gap check

Branch m4/series-deadtime at 926560b, repo venv, through the package's own functions (`deadtime._Fit`,
`_fit_tau`, `_profile_interval`, `calibrate_deadtime_series`, `calibrate_dark`, `forward.check_dead_time_gaps`).
Probes are in `scratchpad/council/r4-dt/`. `cov.py` + `summ.py` reproduce the per-step intervals (the
global both-branch profile "g", the fit held to the low branch "h", the joined interval "j"). These are
profile-only intervals, before the afterpulse widening, so compare them with each other, not with 95%.
Regimes: pq = the CI passive-quench regime (tau_p 40, tau_np 45 ns, 2%/30 ns afterpulsing, 1/3/6 Mcps,
160 reps); s30 = the CHANGELOG's own example (tau_p 30 ns, 1/3/10 Mcps, 96 reps); past = round 3's N2
scan (1/3/60 Mcps, tau_p 40, 48 reps).

## Checked and found sound

- Gap threshold false refusals (the implementer's change from the council's 0.9 x lower end): the change is right. The plain 95% lower end misses the truth in 2.4-5.7% of dark runs (5% by design when afterpulsing is not ruled out), so 0.9 x lower would refuse real detectors whenever 0.9 does not rescue. With the 3-width floor: 0/2054 identified dark artifacts had the truth below the floor. A false refusal also needs at least 10 analysed gaps below 0.9 x floor. Estimated false-refusal rate is about 1e-4 per channel or less (e^{-9} to e^{-9.7} tail).
- Gap check at low rates: a 1 us artifact on a 30 ns detector at 2-5 kcps passes because of `GAP_SHARE`. The missed live-fraction error is 0.2-0.5%, so the share rule roughly ties refusal to material dead fractions. Reasonable.
- S2 (two-mode survival tail): matches a 90-digit `decimal` series to a relative 4.4e-8 or better for n tau in {0.2, 0.5, 0.9, 0.99, 1, 1.01, 1.1, 2, 3}, w up to 40 tau (`surv.py`).
- S1 (per-run interruption check), S3 (cell-averaged kernel), S4 (pending note), S6/D8 (cap at tau_np), S7 (upper-limit sensitivity) and N3 (int tau): the code matches the rulings (deadtime.py:586-599, :544, :616-621; base.py:53-66). Each has a test in test_calibration_deadtime.py. The cap is applied after the afterpulse widening, so it also bounds the inflated intervals of #1 at tau_np.
- N2 (past-maximum run): fixed in substance. The global fit covers 45/48 in the past regime, against 0/48 for the low branch alone (the old behaviour). Coverage there rests on the global fit, not on the join.
- The both-branch profile is the correct profile likelihood: Newton from both branch starts, minimum kept per run. Asimov shows that held and both-branch profiles agree wherever the low branch is lower.
- Without afterpulsing, holding the low branch is exact: the law depends on n only through a = n e^{-n tau}, and the low branch covers every a. The p = 0 "either rate" note is correct.
- Limit (not a finding): the profile-only intervals undercover in s30 (held 86/96) before the afterpulse widening. Whether the full calibration covers there was not run. 1e7 cps has a pending fraction of 0.46%, just under the note threshold.
