## Checked and found sound

- **Multi-pair emission (CW):** pairs from different emissions in the window are the accidentals that the
  same run's off-peak windows measure, to order tau_c / W. Nothing first-order is missing for CW. It is
  first-order for pulsed light (#3).
- **Efficiency mismatch:** HOM V and fringe V are ratios on one detector pair, so it cancels. In CHSH it does not
  cancel, and that is labelled (round 2 B4). Basis and polarization errors belong to the source and analysers.
  Correcting them would be wrong, and the code does not try.
- **Timing walk and drift:** for CHSH, the calibrated delays are compared with the analysed peaks through the
  window capture (round 2 B1). For HOM and fringe the peak is refitted per run. Walk at different rates between
  the dip and reference runs is negligible (the singles barely change).
- **Background drift:** the accidentals come from each acquisition's own off-peak windows. Drift slower than the
  guard cancels, as accidentals.py says. Pump drift between acquisitions is labelled (TWO_POINT; fringe H1).
- **Single-channel live fraction** 1 - m·tau is exact for non-paralyzable Poisson input. The pair term is the
  only approximation (#5). Ignoring tau_p is second order and was stated (D4).
- **Series dead-time model placement:** an illuminated gap histogram is the right instrument for tau_np (to the
  tick) and the only one for tau_p. Its value to the forward model is chiefly tau_np, which the analysed run also
  gives (#4). tau_p matters for extrapolating to other rates and above 20% dead fraction. P-then-NP fits a
  diode or front-end recovery followed by electronics hold-off; the AQ reset-window order and soft SNSPD recovery
  are already stated limits (round 1 D2, D3). Keeping it out of the forward model until M5 is right.
- **The T3 tick as a dead-time bound** (res + 1 with rounding) is right for tau_np widening: dead-time gaps
  cross syncs, so the comb does not hold there. Only its reuse as a histogram lattice is wrong (#1, #2).
- **The T3 cross-sync offset** (period mod res, up to res/2) needs no model at 125k coincidences per histogram
  (`t3hi.txt`).
- **Pulsed T3 refusal (round 7 Fix 3)** works as ruled. The gap is T2 and plain tags (#3).
