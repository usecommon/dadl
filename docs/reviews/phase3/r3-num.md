## Checked and found sound

- **`survival` (series plus two Lambert-W modes, expm1 divided difference)**: checked against an exact 120-digit Decimal sum for n tau_p from 1e-3 to 20, w up to 3,000 tau_p, and tau_p = 1 ps and 20 ns. The relative error is at most 2.4e-15, except near n tau_p = 1, where it is at most 5.1e-8 (the sqrt(eps) of `lambertw` at the branch point, as round 1 found). No overflow or underflow at the extremes (`p1_surv.py`).
- **Newton in log n, without the branch issue**: from a cold start (1/mean) the NLL is within 3e-5 of a 1D reference optimum for series-spad, a high-rate scan and passive-quench, at tau_p 0, 0.3x, 1x, 0.9 tau_np and tau_np (`p2_newton.py`).
- **Profile maximisation and bracketing**: `_fit_tau` matches a 400-point dense profile to within 0.005 NLL. The interval ends agree with the dense profile's to about the grid spacing. The profile is unimodal, and the warm cache changes it by 1e-6 or less. Tested with tau_p 2 ns, 20 ns and tau_p = tau_np (`p3_profile.py`). Coverage of tau_p at the series-spad truth: 379/400 (`p4_smalltp.py`).
- **Afterpulse kernel**: `fftconvolve` agrees with a direct convolution to 1e-15 of the peak. The afterpulse correction integrates to |1e-4| or less (times p). This holds for tau_a from 5 ns to 1 us and rates from 1e5 to 2e7 (`p5_kernel.py`).
- **`grid_capture`**: exact against the closed form for sigma → 0 at offsets inside, at and beyond W. Cancellation appears only at sigma/W ≥ 1e4 (relative error 1.3e-8), which is unphysical (`p11_capture.py`).
- **`_g`, `_ShapeModel.mu`**: the second difference loses at most about |z| eps (s/tick) in absolute terms, which is negligible at realistic tick, sigma and W. Where the peak is resolved, the fitted offsets of `pooled_peak` are within 1-3 sd (`p6_pooled.py`, 350 ps jitter).
- **`propagate.draws`**: one shared row index per artifact, seeded on both 32-bit halves of the seed and the artifact hash. `forward._dead_times` and `_capture` call it with the same seed, so the draws stay joint.
