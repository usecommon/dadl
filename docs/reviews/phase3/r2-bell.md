## Checked and found sound

- Headline stays raw; the corrected value is secondary with "not a Bell claim" in the label and the CLI line; every CHSH correction needs `Policy.allow`; `threshold_labels` gets both values.
- A partial delay-jitter set (not all four pairs) or a channel without a dark artifact is refused (ValueError), as the CHANGELOG says.
- Unequal dead times across the four channels: the per-combination live fraction with min(tau) in the conditional term behaves as for equal detectors (p1: covered 6/6).
- A delay error on one channel only cancels in E for symmetric counts (p8: A+ 400 ps, corrected z -0.30); the damage needs correlated errors as in finding 1.
- A misdeclared window delay with a current delay artifact is corrected by the capture term (p9: 1.986 [1.966, 2.007]).
- Off-peak accidentals at >= 10 us shifts sit well clear of SPAD dead times and afterpulse ranges used here; with afterpulsing SPADs the dark dead time is identified (p4: bounds 22-26 ns for 22 ns), and corrected S at 1-2e6 pairs/s covers.
- Calibration-on-analysed-data refusal, tagger_* / bias / temperature condition checks and validity windows in `propagate.py` work as documented (code reading; not re-probed).
