| 1 | questionable | The one-sided ("at least 95%") rule for `dead_time_ps` hides a real, if tiny, simulator/estimator mismatch, and its stated reason is wrong. The docstring and CHANGELOG say "tens of thousands of gaps fall per tick at the edge, so the shortest gap is almost always tau_np itself". Measured: the edge density is **1.5 gaps per tick** (series-spad) and **0.71** (passive-quench). The shortest gap equals tau_np in **78.5%** and **45%** of replicates. The interval is the exact discrete quantile interval `[m - k_hi, m]` with k_hi = 2 and 4 ticks, so its design coverage can be computed exactly: **97.7%** and **95.8%**. Observed: **99.2%** (1000 reps, z = +3.2) and **97.25%** (400 reps). The cause is a half-tick convention. `_dead_time` assumes P(D <= k) = 1 - exp(-lambda(k + 1/2)). Effect on numbers: at most 1 ps on 45-50 ns, so this is not wrong physics. But the gate has almost no power here. Scaling the model edge density f0 by 0.25 to 3 leaves coverage at 97.6% or higher (pass). ## Checked and found sound

- **Bands.** `gate(1000, k)` = 92.6-97.1% (k=1), 92.4-97.2% (k=2), 92.3-97.3% (k=4). Exact false-fail at p = 0.95 is 8.0e-4, 3.7e-4 and 2.0e-4, within FALSE_FAIL/k. Power at 1000 replicates: a true coverage of 90% or 98% fails essentially always. 93% passes 72-79% of the time and 94% passes 97%. So the gate catches roughly 3-point miscalibration, not 1-point. That is inherent to n = 1000 and worth stating as a limit.
- **Misfit and flag caps.** Coverage 1% cap: false-fail is about 0 at the nominal 0.1-0.2%, and 1.4% at 0.5%. Propagation 2% artifact cap: 1.1% false-fail if the true rate were 1.2%. But measured chsh-dead-time (8 artifacts per replicate) is 0.6% (2/320), where the false-fail is about 0. Series-spad misfit share 0.3%, passive-quench 0/400.
- **Control has power.** chsh-dead-time over 320 replicates (seed 20261118): corrected 95.3%, control 86.6%, so the probability the control lands below 92.6% at n = 1000 is about 1. Calibration is 26% of the interval variance. A bug that halves the calibration component brings coverage to about 88% (approximate symmetric recomputation from recorded intervals) and would fail. The corrected S is 0.37 sd low on average (consistent with the CHANGELOG's 0.26 +- 0.29 sigma).
- **Misspecified regime.** 0.1% crosstalk is an easy point: 100% flagged in my runs. At weaker crosstalk, 0.04% gives 100% flagged, 0.02% gives 46%, 0.01% gives 2% (100 replicates each). Coverage among *unflagged* replicates stays near nominal (92.6% at 0.02%, 94.9% at 0.01%). So the check's blind spot does not produce badly wrong intervals in this probe. Gating the coverage of unflagged replicates at a weaker point would make this a standing guarantee (optional).
- **Truths come from outside the estimators.** Dead time and tau_p are the injected DetectorParams. HOM truth is `closed_form.hom_visibility_expected`, which neither sim.py nor forward.py uses. CHSH truth is `chsh_ideal`, which shares only the `SIGNS` definition with the estimator; the simulator draws outcomes on its own.
- **Seeding.** Replicate i of each regime is `SeedSequence(seed, spawn_key=(crc32(regime), i))`, so results do not depend on jobs, chunks or shards. Regimes are independent, including hom-bright vs hom-crosstalk. Draw seeds are mixed with the input hashes (`draws_rng`), so the shared `fit_seed` across channels in `propagation._calibrate` does not correlate artifacts.
- **Shard and merge.** Both merges refuse mixed seeds or totals, gaps, overlaps, duplicates and partial tiling, each checked per result name. Tests cover equality with the whole run and the refusals. CI runs every declared regime: coverage matrix, passive-quench in 4 shards, propagation matrix, chsh-dead-time in 8 shards.
- **The plan's band number** (92.6-97.1% for one quantity) matches `gate(1000, 1)`. The "300-replicate pilot covered 100%" claim holds on the CI seed (first 300 replicates: 100%).
