# Hardware twin, end to end (a first-time lab user with PTU files)

Probes and data: `scratchpad/council/r9-twin/`. The twin has its own physics, written from scratch and independent of
`dadl.sim`: `twin/twinlib.py` and `twin/gen_bell.py` / `gen_hom.py`. Only the PTU record encoders and the header-entry packer
come from DADL. The PTU headers look like recordings: `CreatorSW_Name` HydraHarp/MultiHarp, no `DADL_Simulated`, CFD and
trigger levels, offsets, enabled flags, module index, and `HWSync_*` entries. The detector chain is: efficiency, detector
jitter, a cable delay of several ns, darks, a series dead time (paralyzable 15 ns, then non-paralyzable about 22 ns),
afterpulsing, and **tagger jitter added after the dead time**.

- **Lab 1** (HydraHarp 400, T2, 1 ps tick, four SPADs on inputs 1-4, CHSH). Delays are 0 / 3170 / 8420 / 5960 ps. The dark
  day is at 293.15 K. The measurement day is 1.5 K warmer, with darks x1.12. There are four heralding runs (one per channel
  pair) and a four-rate dead-time scan on A+. Truth: S = 2.6889 with the efficiency imbalance (2.6870 at the source).
- **Lab 2** (MultiHarp 160, T3, 5 ps, an external 20 MHz clock on sync measured as 49999.37 ps, two SNSPDs, CW SPDC, HOM).
  B's delay is 4372 ps. The cryostat is at 2.20 K on the dark day and 2.35 K on the measurement day, with darks x1.4.
  Truth: V = 0.92.

Workflow, docs only: `dadl init` for both examples, then edit setup.yaml (`backend: file`, `hardware`, `channels` from 1),
`calibrate dark`, `calibrate pairs` (probe, then declare the delays, then the real run), `calibrate deadtime --dark`, and
`analyze --calibrations`. Exact commands are in the shell history of the two sub-folders `bell/` and `hom/`; artifacts are
in `bell/art`, `hom/art` and `hom/art_nodark`.

| 1 | wrong | **T3 delay calibration is biased by pairs that straddle a sync edge, and its interval excludes the truth with status ok.** `calibrate pairs` bins pair delays on the dtime lattice (`lattice_ps`). Within one sync that is exact. Across a sync edge, a difference sits off the comb by (period mod resolution), and the fit floors it into the wrong lattice bin. The bias is about −(delay / period) × (period mod res). It is negligible for delays near 0 (the regime round 8 tested), but realistic cable delays of a few ns put 9-35% of pairs across the edge. | `probes/res80.py`, `probes/resN.py`; outputs `probes/res80.txt`, `probes/res25.txt`. ## Checked and found sound (against the injected truth)

- **Dark, Lab 1, 30 s, four SPADs.** Dark rate 421 / 605 / 378 / 524 against 420 / 610 / 380 / 520. Afterpulse p
  0.0054-0.0070 against 0.006, with every interval covering. tau_a 50-72 ns against 60, covered. Dead time: every interval
  covers 21.8-22.3 ns.
- **Dark, Lab 2, T3 with a non-integer period.** Dark rate 81 / 138 against 80 / 140. Dead time reported correctly as an
  upper limit (6.4 us and 48.7 ns), with a clear note.
- **Pairs, Lab 1, four pairs.** Delays 8418.5 / 5957.0 / 5251.0 / 2792.1 ps against 8420 / 5960 / 5250 / 2790, all
  covered. Jitter 253.7-256.6 ps against 254.95, covered. Heralding / capture 0.147 / 0.135 / 0.145 / 0.133, matching
  coupling × efficiency after herald dark dilution. The probe run with undeclared delays gave clear guidance ("declared
  delay differs ... by 8419 ps").
- **Pairs, Lab 2, T3 5 ps.** Delay 4371.73 [4371.35, 4372.11] against 4372. Jitter 39.84 against 40. Heralding / capture
  0.199 against 0.200. Pair rate 2.02e5 against 2.0e5.
- **Dead-time scan, A+.** tau_p 14.87 [14.48, 15.24] ns against 15. Candidate rates against truth: 1.036 Mcps for
  2 Mcps × 0.52. tau_np 21971 [21970, 21972] against 22000: the 29 ps offset is the documented tagger-jitter edge
  (README: about 4√2 σ_tagger; 10 ps here), not a new finding. Running all channels names the three dark ones with "need at
  least 1000 gaps", which is clear.
- **CHSH.** Raw S 2.7064 ± 0.0193 and corrected S 2.7072 ± 0.0195 [2.667, 2.745] against 2.6889. Forward check p 0.97,
  off-peak p 0.68, drift z −1.17, gap floors "lower end (label only)" (the floor is negative for a 30 s dark run at
  400-600 cps, as documented). A B+ fibre re-seat of +150 ps on the measurement day was neither refused nor biased beyond
  noise (corrected 2.664 ± 0.020, drift z 1.72).
- **HOM.** Raw V 0.9183 ± 0.0035 and corrected V 0.9194 ± 0.0034 against 0.92. The T3 rounding note and the "T3 input: CW"
  label are present. Forward check p 1.0 on 5 ps T3 with a 4.4 ns delay; the 5 ps bias of #1 is about 0.3 ps, within noise.
- **Guidance that did its job.** The refusals for a missing `--allow`, for `backend: sim` with recorded files, and for the
  scan's missing condition, and the source-on check on dark data, all said what to do.
