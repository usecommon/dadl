## Checked and found sound

- Artifact IDs are reproducible across processes: `deadtime-series` gave the same ID and byte-identical `.npy` in a fresh process with `SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH` and `PYTHONHASHSEED` set; `SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH` is deliberately ignored. Corrected HOM records were identical across two processes.
- v0.1.1-written dark and delay-jitter artifacts read, verify and load in HEAD; dark.py and delay.py are unchanged since v0.1.1 (method versions 7 and 8), and the posteriors are byte-identical.
- `schema/` and `python/dadl/schemas/` copies are identical. A `valid_for` without `from`/`to` falls back to acquired/created and runs open-ended (propagate.py:211).
- Records cite only the artifacts the model read. The HOM record omits the excluded dark artifacts, and the CHSH record lists four delay-jitter IDs in sorted, deterministic order. Records carry the policy fingerprint, overrides with reasons, corrections in order, the dataset context, capture offsets, `delay_drift_*`, and `min_gap_ps`/`gap_check_floor_ps` when dark artifacts are used.
- Joint draws are keyed on (seed, artifact ID), and directory members are loaded in sorted order, so the draws do not depend on file order.
- `deadtime-series` artifacts are kept out of HOM/fringe/CHSH sets (and their `artifact_ids`), and the set is labelled `SERIES_UNUSED`.
