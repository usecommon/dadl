## Checked and found sound

- T3 decoding against the independent test writer: nsync wrap at 1022-1025, 2047/2048 and 65535-65537, and a jump to 5e6 syncs. Tags round-trip exactly on hydraharp (hh2 multi-count overflows), picoharp (76 single overflows) and multiharp150n (80 ps). Markers near the wrap read at their sync (documented). Record accounting adds up (`edges.py`).
- Non-whole period arithmetic: `_t3_to_ps` splits the period with `Fraction` (integer whole ps plus a float fraction), so there is no drift over 1e12 syncs. The rounding note and `exact_ps: False` are set.
- The T3 time model note (sync period assumed constant): an error f in the measured period shifts absolute times by f x t. Same-sync coincidences are unaffected, and cross-sync gaps are off by f x gap (1e-7 x 30 ns, negligible). Stated.
- Input numbering: HydraHarp family k+1 with sync at 0, the same in T2 and T3. The derived T3 sync channel is dropped unless mapped. A divided sync mapped as a detector is refused. Unknown special records are refused in `read_tags`. Incomplete stop reasons are refused.
- Detector vs tagger dead time: the series artifact measures the effective shortest recorded gap (detector and tagger dead time in series), the docs say so (round 1 D5 limit), and the simulator states that TDC dead time is not simulated (sim.py:37). This is fine as long as the tagger identity is kept (#6).
- T2 ticks: `tag_tick_ps` from `MeasDesc_GlobalResolution` is right for PicoHarp 4 ps, HydraHarp 1 ps, TimeHarp 25 ps and MultiHarp 5/80 ps. The round-6 widening works there (59/60 on the whole-multiple grid).
