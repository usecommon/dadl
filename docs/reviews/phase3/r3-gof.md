`scratchpad/council/r3-gof/` (outputs `out_*.txt`). Physics, unless stated: the `r2-bell` CHSH setup

## Checked and found sound

- Forward check null in the gate's CHSH regimes (`p2_gate.py`, 300 replicates each of chsh-spad and chsh-dead-time, gate seeds): 0 predictive-check flags and 0 drift flags; smallest Bonferroni p 0.0015. The gate's 1% flag cap holds there.
- `_grouped_pearson` on (groups - 1) dof: the shape model is linear in its three amplitudes, so the Poisson MLE has sum(mu) = sum(n). The statistic is then bounded by chi2(groups - 1), as the docstring says.
- Dead-time Pearson null (`p3_deadtime_null.py`, 400 coverage replicates each). series-spad: uniform (KS p 0.59); P(p < 0.01) = 1.5%, P(p < 0.05) = 4.25%. passive-quench: 1.1% and 4.8%, slightly conservative in the body (median 0.53, KS p 0.006). The tail is nominal, not "conservative" as the docstring suggests, which does no harm. groups - 1 rather than groups - (runs) is conservative by a few dof out of thousands.
- Interruption test null per run (`p8_int.py`, 2000 runs each, series-spad detector at 2e6, 5e6 and 1e7 cps, series, non-paralyzable and no dead time): P(p < 0.001) between 0.000 and 0.002, P(p < 0.05) between 0.046 and 0.055, so nominal. The 3/400 series-spad `ppc < 0.001` all come from the interruption part: 5e6 cps runs whose Poisson input really had a 4.0-4.3 µs gap (mean 200 ns). Their p of 5e-5 to 1.4e-4 is correct for that gap (`p9_which.py`, `p10_where.py`). The input generator is fine over 20,000 draws (`p11_rng.py`: 5 above 4 µs against 4.1 expected). Bonferroni over runs and the 2 x min combination are correct.
- Drift check power (current rule): 100 ps at 150 ps / 1 ns 30/30 refused, 200 ps 20/20; 200 ps at 350 ps / 2 ns 17/20 (the other 3 refused by the predictive check). G1's fix keeps all of these.
- Raw capture balance on matched delays at W >= 1.5 ns: (raw - reweighted)/se at most 0.30 (20 seeds); `test_matched_delays_are_not_labelled` holds.
