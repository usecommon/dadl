# First-time user advocate (round 10)

I worked as a new graduate student would, using only README.md, python/README.md and `--help`. I ran
`dadl init` (both examples), `dadl quickstart` (both), and simulate, calibrate dark, calibrate pairs and
analyze with and without `--calibrations`, for hom-snspd and chsh-spad. I used the venv, and all output
is in `r10-ux/` (`hom/a*.txt`, `chsh/a*.txt`, `qs-*.txt`).

Outcome: I got an honestly labelled corrected HOM V in 5 analyze attempts (3 refusals). I got a
corrected CHSH S in 4 attempts (2 refusals, plus one dead end that needed a workaround nobody tells
you about). The README's one-line CHSH recipe leads straight into that dead end. Each refusal is
correct, but they arrive one per run, and two of them do not say what to do. The printed output is
honest but flat. CHSH analyze prints 14 lines and up to 17 semicolon-separated caveats. The longest
line is 528 characters. In that line an override I set myself ("calibration outside its validity")
comes 6th of 7 items, next to routine bookkeeping. No single place lists every label with its meaning
and what to do.

## What I would misquote in a lab report

- I would put "policy modified" next to the raw S or V (finding 2).
- I could not name the overrides I used or their reasons, because analyze prints only a hash (finding 1).
- I would write "± 0.0105 (95%)" for the corrected value, and "calibration uncertainty 6e-06, negligible" (finding 11).
- On CHSH without delay-jitter artifacts, I would imply that a model check passed (finding 7).
- Sound as it stands: I would not mistake the corrected value for the headline ("secondary; not a Bell claim" appears twice). The "Coincidences per singles" line, beside the "fair sampling" label, makes it clear the detection loophole is open.

## Checked and found sound

- `dadl init` prints clear next steps for simulated and recorded data, including the `backend: file` and `hardware:` edits.
- Both quickstarts run in about 5 s and print raw, corrected and closed-form values. HOM: raw 0.9186, corrected 0.9198, truth 0.92. CHSH: raw 2.694, corrected 2.692, closed form 2.686.
- The headline is always raw. The corrected value is marked secondary on its own line, and the exact 95% interval on raw V is printed.
- An `--allow` typo is refused with the full list of known names.
- Policy refusals name the exact `--allow` flag. The dead-fraction refusal names the remedy (move the dark artifacts out, each `.json` with its `.posterior.npy`), and following it worked.
- The dark calibration's SNSPD false identification (20 µs "identified" on a 30 ns detector) is warned at calibration and refused at analysis. The output is honest, if alarming.
- The simulated-data label appears on every analyze of simulator tags. The "declare before acquisition" warning cites a concrete false-violation rate.
