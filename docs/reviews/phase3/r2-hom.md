## Checked and found sound

- **Declared reference delay, clean data.** V is unbiased: 30 runs (W300, ref 80) mean z +0.10, coverage 30/30. Four geometries (sigma 25-40 ps per detector, W 200-300, ref 15-80) gave coverage 0.93 each over 40 runs and no false flags in 160 runs.
- **Misdeclared reference delays are caught.** Undeclared 40, 80 and 150 ps, and 80 ps declared as 40, were all flagged (8/8 each). The reported V would have been 3-21 sigma low.
- **In-window crosstalk is caught when a delay-jitter artifact is loaded.** 0.3% and 1% each way, at 0, 30 and 120 ps, were flagged 20/20. V would have been 5-14 sigma low.
- **SNSPD timing tails do not bias V.** With 3-10% tails of 100-300 ps the corrected V stays unbiased (mean z between -0.5 and +0.3), because the capture largely cancels in the ratio when d << W.
- **The forward interval covers at low counts** (Poisson counts, 1,500 reps each, 200 off-peak shifts):
  - HOM: 0.945-0.955, down to 30 reference / 2 dip counts.
  - Fringe: 0.956 at 100 / 5 counts, 0.933 at 40 / 2.
- **Accidentals and exposure.** Each acquisition's accidentals come from its own off-peak bins, the exposure is each acquisition's span, and pulsed sources are refused in config. The ±d equal mixture is right for a 50:50 splitter. The 2RT/(R²+T²) cap and the singles-at-dip factor (η − η²/2)/(η − η²/4) check out analytically.
- **Refusing subtraction together with calibrations** prevents counting the accidentals twice.
