# Metrology: quantities, units, uncertainty statements (phase 3, series dead time)

All probes ran on HEAD 7bcc32b with the repo venv, through the package's own CLI and functions. They are in
`scratchpad/council/r6-metro/`:
- `tick.py`: continuous-time hard-edge detector, tags floored to a tick, then `calibrate_deadtime_series`.
- `pq.py`: one passive-quench replicate, the record's budget fields, and a refit with the afterpulse draws collapsed.
- `corr.py`: p and tau_a correlation in the dark draws.
- CLI runs: `simulate chsh-spad --deadtime-scan` / `--dark`, `calibrate dark`, `calibrate deadtime` (folders `dtcal`, `dtcal30`, `darkcal25`).

## Findings

| # | Severity | Finding | Evidence | Where | Suggested fix |

| 1 | questionable | **The tau_np interval collapses to zero width and leaves out the tag quantisation.** At high edge density, k_lo = k_hi = 0, so the interval is [m, m]. Real taggers floor continuous times to a tick. The difference of two floored tags lies within (-1, +1) tick of the true gap. When tau_np is not a whole number of ticks, the shortest recorded gap is the floor, which lies below tau_np. The interval then excludes the truth every time. The half-tick law in `_dead_time` assumes the true dead time sits on the tick grid, as it does in the simulator. The coverage gate cannot see this, because the simulator uses an integer dead time and 1 ps ticks. ## Checked and found sound

- Units: the `units` map gives ps for both estimates; `PS_PER_S` conversions in the notes are right (fitted 2.275e6/s = 0.5 x 4.55e6 photons/s).
- A limit-only tau_p is written with `value: null` and `bound: upper`, not as a point (artifact.py:131-148).
- The identified tau_p interval combines its statistical half-widths with 1.96 x the afterpulse shift sd in quadrature, side by side (asymmetric), GUM-style. The shift sd uses the full (p, tau_a) covariance of the dark draws.
- The split-normal draws match the interval: draw quantiles [28528, 41956] against the interval [28397, 41882] on the probe replicate. Draws and interval are capped at tau_np consistently.
- The union-of-branches interval is described in a note with both component intervals.
- `afterpulse_prob_held`, `afterpulse_tau_ps_held` and their sds are recorded, so the held inputs are traceable (round-5 fix present).
- The tau_np quantile interval switches to one-sided 95% when k_lo = 0 and keeps `level` 0.95 in both cases. That is consistent: problem 1 concerns the grid assumption, not the level.
